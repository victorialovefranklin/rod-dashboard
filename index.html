Processing fee

A processing fee may apply. Requests that take more than one hour of staff time to locate, retrieve, review, redact, or reproduce are charged for labor. Copies are $0.50 per page ($1.00 certified); plat-size copies are $3.00 ($5.00 certified). There is no duplication charge for requests under 5 black-and-white pages. Flash drives and outside vendor work are charged at actual cost. You'll receive an itemized estimate before any work begins, and payment is due in advance by cash, personal check payable to Davidson County Register of Deeds, money order, or credit or debit card. Copy requests require proof of Tennessee citizenship, such as a valid Tennessee driver's license.

Sending opens your email app. If it doesn't open, email the request to rod.infodesk@nashville.gov.
